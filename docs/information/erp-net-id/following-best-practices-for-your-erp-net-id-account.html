Following Best Practices for Your ERP.net ID Account

Use your ERP.net ID account consistently to support smoother sign-in, clearer account identification and management, and better access continuity across supported ERP.net apps and services.

Overview

Good account practices help keep your ERP.net ID account clear, reliable, and ready to use across the ERP.net ecosystem.

They are especially important when you create and activate your account, start using it across supported apps and services, and coordinate access to ERP.net instances.

This guide explains what matters when creating and managing your ERP.net ID account and which practices help you use it securely and consistently.

Best Practices for ERP.net ID Account Management

1. Choose the Right Email Address

Before creating your ERP.net ID account, choose your email address carefully.

Important
We recommend using your official individual email address within your organization.

Your email address helps identify your account across ERP.net services.

Use an inbox you can access and manage. This helps you receive confirmation messages, access notifications, and account-related communication.

Your ERP.net ID account is intended to represent you personally, so make sure to use an email address that clearly belongs to you as an individual user.

2. Complete Your Profile Information

The Profile tab contains optional fields, such as:

  • Phone number
  • Default language
  • Company
  • Registration message

Although these fields are optional, we strongly recommend filling them in.

Profile information can help administrators, support teams, and service owners understand your account context when access, communication, or support is needed.

The Company field is especially useful when your account needs to be associated with a specific organization.

Use the Registration message field when extra context is useful, such as the organization, service, or access context related to your registration.

3. Confirm Your Email Address Promptly

Confirm your email address promptly after registration so your account becomes active without delay.

Email confirmation activates your ERP.net ID account and verifies that your email address can be used for account-related communication.

If you do not see the confirmation email:

  • check your spam or junk folder;
  • confirm that the email address you entered is correct;
  • request a new confirmation email from the ERP.net ID confirmation screen.

4. Keep Individual Accounts Personal

Each ERP.net ID account should belong to the person it represents.

Personal accounts support:

  • clear ownership;
  • reliable access coordination;
  • accurate communication.

Since employee access to the ERP.net ecosystem is personal and user-specific, each person should use their own ERP.net ID account.

5. Protect Your Sign-In Credentials

Keep your ERP.net ID credentials private.

Use a strong, unique password.

Do not:

  • reuse passwords from other services;
  • share your sign-in details with another person.

Store your credentials securely and update them promptly if you suspect they may have been exposed.

6. Use One ERP.net ID Account Consistently

Use the same ERP.net ID account wherever ERP.net ID is supported.

This keeps your sign-in, email address, and profile information aligned across supported apps, services, and enabled ERP.net instances.

If you accidentally create more than one ERP.net ID account, coordinate with your administrator, support contact, or service owner to determine which account you should continue using.

7. Coordinate ERP.net Instance Access When Needed

An active ERP.net ID account gives you a sign-in identity, while each ERP.net app, service, or instance controls access according to its own settings, roles, and permission rules.

If you need access to a specific ERP.net instance, your organization may need to enable ERP.net ID login and assign the required roles and permissions to your account.

Contact your ERP.net administrator or responsible service owner if you:

  • can sign in with ERP.net ID but cannot access the needed ERP.net instance;
  • need additional permissions and roles;
  • need help with connecting your account to a specific organization, service, or business context.

Note
A successful sign-in confirms your identity. Permissions to view or change business data depend on the relevant ERP.net app, service, instance, and assigned access rights.

Summary and Additional Resources

Following good ERP.net ID account practices enables you to use your account clearly, securely, and professionally across supported ERP.net apps and services.

By choosing the right email address, completing useful profile details, confirming your account, protecting your credentials, and using the same ERP.net ID account consistently, you support smoother sign-in, clearer account management, and better access continuity across the ERP.net ecosystem.
